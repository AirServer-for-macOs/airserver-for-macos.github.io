How It Works

Step 1: Install and Launch

AirServer is pre-activated. Open the app on your Mac—it runs quietly in the background, ready to receive.

Step 2: Enable AirPlay or Google Cast

On your iPhone, iPad, Android, or other device, select your Mac from the AirPlay or Cast menu in Control Center or app settings.

Step 3: Stream or Mirror

Choose audio, video, photos, slideshows, or full screen mirroring. Content appears instantly on your Mac's display.

Step 4: Manage Multiple Streams

Handle simultaneous casts effortlessly. Adjust settings within AirServer for optimal quality and control.
